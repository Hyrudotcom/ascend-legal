Getting started

On first launch you'll pick a level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced). The Today screen then shows your prescribed session. Tap Start, log each set as you go, and tap Finish when you're done — Gravity works out your next session automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How does the progression work?

Gravity calculates each session from your last one. If you hit your targets, it increases the challenge; if you fall short, it adjusts so you can keep training productively.

Do I need an account?

An account (email + password) lets your training sync across devices and stay backed up. The app also works offline and syncs automatically when you're back online.

I didn't get my confirmation email.

Check your spam folder. Confirmation emails can take a few minutes. If it still doesn't arrive, contact us.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Reminders are optional weekly local notifications. You can allow or deny them when prompted, and change this any time in your device's notification settings for Gravity.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, tap your avatar on Home, open Settings, then choose Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your training data. You can also email us to request deletion.

Is Gravity free?

Yes — Gravity is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
