Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity ("we", "us", the "App") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Gravity mobile application. We are the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you have any questions, contact us at hyrudotcom@icloud.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used to create and secure your account. Your password is handled and stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text.
  • Training data (health & fitness): the workouts you log — exercises, sets, repetitions, loads, effort ratings, dates, and your selected program and progress.
  • Account identifier: a unique user ID that links your training data to your account.
  • Optional photos: a profile photo you choose to sync with your training state, and park photos you choose to submit for community moderation and display.
  • Optional community contributions: park comments, ratings, proposed park locations or edits (including submitted map coordinates and notes), and feedback you send to the team.
  • Crash diagnostics: when crash reporting is enabled, technical crash details, app breadcrumbs, app version, operating-system and device information used to diagnose reliability problems. Gravity does not attach your account email or user ID to Sentry crash reports.

Location access is optional. Your current location is used on your device to center the park map. Gravity does not upload your current location merely because you view the map; coordinates are sent to our backend only when you choose to submit a park or park edit. Set videos recorded during a workout are saved to your device library and are not uploaded by Gravity.

We do not collect contacts, financial information, emails or SMS messages, audio, calendar data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

2. How and why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the App (create your account, store and sync your training across your devices): processing necessary for the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Optional reminders (local notifications): based on your device permission, i.e. your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can revoke this at any time in your device settings.
  • Community features and feedback (photos, comments, park submissions, ratings, and feedback): to provide the feature you requested and operate/moderate the service (Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Crash diagnosis: to maintain app reliability and security, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Security and integrity of the service: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Storage and processors

Your data is stored locally on your device and, when you sign in, synced to our backend provider Supabase (PostgreSQL database and authentication), which processes data on our behalf as a data processor. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Technical crash reports are processed by Sentry on our behalf. Map imagery and tiles are provided by MapTiler; requests for the geographic area displayed on the map are sent to MapTiler, subject to its role as our service provider.

4. Retention

We keep account-linked data for as long as your account exists, except where a shorter period applies or retention is required for security or legal reasons. When you delete your account, your account and associated training state and user-owned contributions are deleted or disassociated from our systems as implemented by the account-deletion process. Crash reports are retained according to our Sentry retention configuration.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You may withdraw consent (e.g. for notifications) at any time.

  • Delete your account and data: in the App, open Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Other requests: email hyrudotcom@icloud.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

6. Children

Gravity is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account through row-level security on our database.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact
